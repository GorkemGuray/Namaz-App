Key Features

Core features designed to make daily use easy and seamless.

Automatic Location-Based Times

Choose between official Diyanet data (Imsakiyem API) or coordinate-based Aladhan API for worldwide calculations. When offline, the mathematical engine calculates times on-device. Times update automatically in the background as your location changes.

Wear OS (Smartwatch) App

Includes a standalone watch version that works independently from your phone. With watch face complications, you can view the remaining time to the next prayer at a glance.

Qibla Compass & Offline Map

Smooth Qibla compass using device sensors. Even without an internet connection, it draws the direction of the Kaaba on the map using the MapLibre library with the great-circle method.

Modern Glance Widgets

Widgets built with the Jetpack Glance library that automatically match your Android system theme. Includes a countdown timer option to track remaining time down to the second.

Namaz Tesbihat & Dhikr Counter

Dhikr counter that automatically guides you through the Namaz Tesbihat steps. Uses the device's haptic feedback (vibration) motor during step transitions and upon completion.

Completely Ad-Free & Private

Contains no ads, third-party tracking, or analytics libraries. Your location is processed strictly on your device to calculate prayer times.
